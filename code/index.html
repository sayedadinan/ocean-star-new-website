About Us

Built on Skill & Passion Since 2023

Ocean Star Auto Maintenance Workshop was established in 2023 with a clear mission: to provide Sharjah drivers with honest, high-quality automotive care they can always count on.

From day one we invested in modern diagnostic equipment and assembled a team of skilled technicians who treat every vehicle — whether a daily commuter or a premium sedan — with the same precision and attention to detail.

“We don't just fix cars. We build lasting relationships with every driver who trusts us with their vehicle.”
